Party Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts party cups, tables and trays with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the cups and packs a guest list takes, the tables, the trays of food, and the cost. It uses no named constants. The guests, the cups and servings a guest, the pack and tray sizes, the seats a table and the prices are yours to decide and read off your own list and receipt. It applies the counting as written, so it has not met your guests and does not know who will come, who will eat what, your venue or any food safety or capacity rule, and it gives no verdict that the food is enough or the room fits. Every number here is your own entry.

The cups truth

Cups: the guests times the cups a guest, divided by the cups a pack, rounded up, with the cups left.

The tables truth

Tables: the guests divided by the seats a table, rounded up, with the empty seats.

The trays truth

Trays: the guests times the servings a guest, divided by the servings a tray, rounded up, with the servings left.

The cost truth

Cost: the packs and the trays, each at their price.
